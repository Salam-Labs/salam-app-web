# Privacy Policy

## About Salam Labs Inc.

Salam Labs Inc. is a registered corporation in Canada.

## What data do we collect?

Salam Labs respects everyone’s right to privacy. The main reason this app exists is to provide a private and safe experience, and so Salam App by default does not collect any data whatsoever.

## Why do we need your location data?

Salam App will only request your location data for prayer time calculations. This is an optional feature; however, note that some features may be limited if disabled. Additionally, location data is only used locally (within your device), and no data is sent to Salam Labs. We recommend updating your location if needed to ensure that prayer time calculations are accurate as possible.

## Optional email address collection

While Salam App is designed to offer a secure and confidential user experience, we may collect email addresses under specific circumstances. Your email address may be collected for the optional utilization of the Salam App Cloud backup system and for account registration using Google authentication. Additionally, in the future, we may extend our services to include newsletters or informational updates, for which we may use your email address to provide you with relevant communications.

You may request data deletion at anytime [here](https://salam.app/data-deletion).

## Do we share data with Third Parties?

No, Salam App does not have any plans to incorporate third-party analytic tools for tracking or collecting user data. Furthermore, any user data we do collect, such as email addresses, will never be shared with any third parties.

## What if there is an update to this policy?

Salam Labs has no plans to update or change our policy in any way that would harm the user. If for any reason there is an update to this policy, we will be explicit in what has changed and notify you the user.

## Links to Other sites

If you click on a third-party link within the app, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policies of these websites. We have no control over, nor do we assume responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

## Contact Us

To get in touch with us, please email us at `feedback@salamlabs.io`.
