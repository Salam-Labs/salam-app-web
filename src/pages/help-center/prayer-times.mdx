# Introduction to Prayer Times

One of its main features as your companion app is the second pillar of Islam , Salah!

> **Indeed, performing prayers is a duty on the believers at the appointed times.** An-Nisa' verse 103

![Prayer settings](https://articles.salam.app/Prayer_Times/4.png)

**_One swipe to the left in Salam App will lead you to the Prayer Times module_**

- At the top of your screen, Salam App presents the countdown to the next prayer in your current timezone.

- First third of the night, midnight and last third of the night are at the bottom.

_you can also read the importance of each by tapping them_

## Personalize your Prayer Notifications

_Note: Prayer Notifications must be enabled from app settings!_

![enter image description here](https://articles.salam.app/Prayer_Times/5.png)

1.  **No Notification**

2.  **Notification only** _if you are at work during Dhuhr time, you can set it to notification only instead of adhaan_

3.  **Notification with adhaan**

## How to Synchronize with your Local Prayer Times

**Very Easy! Go to settings, and toggle "Auto Detect"**

![enter image description here](https://articles.salam.app/Prayer_Times/0.png)**You can also choose calculation method which covers your area**

![](https://articles.salam.app/Prayer_Times/1.png)

**You can also choose Asr calculation based on your Madhab (school of thought)**

If you are a revert and hasn't decide a Madhab (school of thought) yet, don't stress it! All of the 4 major school of thoughts are correct. Except for the Hanafi madhab where they have a slight difference in Asr prayer time.

![enter image description here](https://articles.salam.app/Prayer_Times/2.png)

## If the prayer times on the app are still slightly different from your local masjid

![enter image description here](https://articles.salam.app/Prayer_Times/3.png)

**You can manually correct it by tapping the "Advanced Settings" button.**
