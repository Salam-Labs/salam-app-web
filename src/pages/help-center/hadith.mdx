# Introduction to Hadith Module

<br />
<br />
**Disclaimer: Many Hadith require context and it is recommended to learn from
Scholars.**

## How to Open the Hadith Module

**Step 1:** tap the Module Button at the bottom of the screen.

![Module Button](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/1.jpeg)

**Step 2:** tap the module that says “Hadith”.

![Hadith Module](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/2.jpeg)

## How to Use the Hadith Module

The Hadith module should look like this:

![Hadith Module](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/3.jpeg)

To open a hadith, tap on any of your liking, and it should lead you to a screen looking like this:

![Hadith Screen](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/4.jpeg)

To open the individual chapters of the hadith, you'll need to tap on anyone of your liking, and the screen should change to this:

![Chapter Screen](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/5.jpeg)

To bookmark a Hadith, tap and hold on it, then tap the “Add Bookmark” button and to remove a bookmark, tap the “Remove Bookmark” button:

![Bookmark Hadith](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/6.jpeg)

Check your bookmarks by tapping the white banner at the top right:

![Bookmarks](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/7.jpeg)

![Bookmarks](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/8.jpeg)

## How to Change the Hadith Settings

The Hadith settings can be accessed inside the main settings or through the hadith module, by tapping the cog in the top right corner, and it should open a sidebar.

![Hadith Settings](https://articles.salam.app/Hadith/9.jpeg)

## Why Reading Hadiths are Important

Hadith are full of knowledge, and are narrations of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) (given that they are trusted) There are also a few about the companions of The Prophet (PBUH). The only way to know what they are full of is by reading them for yourself!
