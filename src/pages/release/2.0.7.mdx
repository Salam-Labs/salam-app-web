## All Platforms

This release focuses on reliability across clock changes, making sure prayer times, countdowns, dates, and reminders stay accurate when daylight saving time begins or ends.

- Fixed prayer time calculations around daylight saving clock changes.
- Corrected the overnight countdown to use the next day’s actual Fajr time.
- Fixed missing or repeated dates in Prayer Tracker and Islamic Calendar.
- Corrected weekly Tracker totals, Hijri date adjustments, upcoming Islamic events, and Ramadan countdowns.
- Fixed daily and custom reminder scheduling across clock changes.
