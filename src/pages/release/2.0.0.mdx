# Version 2.0.0 🎉🎉

## All Platforms

This release rebuilds Salam App from the 1.0 experience with a refreshed interface, improved reliability, expanded content systems, and a smoother daily workflow across prayer, Quran, hadith, tafsir, dhikr, reminders, and settings.

- Rebuilt app foundation for better stability, maintainability, and future updates.
- Refreshed interface across the home screen, modules page, navigation, settings, onboarding, and dialogs.
- Expanded language support with language selection during onboarding.
- Optional glass-style containers and refreshed adaptive prayer-time themes.
- More consistent haptic feedback across interactive elements.
- Improved backup and restore for tracker data, Quran bookmarks, favorite surahs, hadith bookmarks, custom dhikr, and preferences.
- Improved Salam App Cloud backup experience.
- Improved Quran reader with better reading modes, search, bookmarks, favorites, tajwid, translations, word-by-word resources, sajda markers, and ruku markers.
- Improved Quran Audio Player with better reciter, subtitle, translation, and download handling.
- Rebuilt tafsir experience with source selection, downloads, and reader improvements.
- Improved hadith reader with better search, bookmarks, and reading settings.
- Improved Dhikr & Dua, reminders, prayer tracker, Islamic calendar, Qibla, 99 Names, charity, and prayer tools.
- Clearer first-launch tours and guidance in key modules.
- Many performance, stability, privacy, and quality-of-life improvements.
